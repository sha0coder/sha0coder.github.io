LLM Benchmark

A reproducible cognitive battery for language models, scored 0–100. Designed to compare small local models against each other and against frontier models, with deterministic grading wherever possible.

Why another benchmark

Public leaderboards are easy to overfit and rarely tell you how a 2–8 GB local model behaves on the kind of reasoning humans are actually tested on. This battery borrows its structure from psychometrics — including subtests inspired by WISC-V and WAIS-IV (digit span, letter–number sequencing, similarities, matrices, mental arithmetic) — and adds domains where correctness is verifiable by code.

The WISC-V / WAIS-IV items are original re-creations of the subtest formats; the real instruments are copyrighted and none of their content is used. The question bank is in Spanish, which also makes it a mild out-of-distribution test for models tuned mainly on English.

Methodology

Every item scores 0–1. Deterministic items are pass/fail; judged items use a rubric-driven LLM judge that returns a 0–10 score with a justification, which is stored for audit. The category score is the mean of its items, and the final score (0–100) is the mean of the seven categories, equally weighted.

  • Fixed judge. All models in a comparison are graded by the same judge model, so judged categories are comparable across runs.
  • Format contract. Models must end with a RESPUESTA: line that graders parse; tolerant extraction and Spanish number formats (72,25) are handled.
  • Backends. The harness speaks to the Anthropic API, Ollama, and the Codex / Claude Code CLIs, so local and frontier models run through the exact same pipeline.

Results

Evaluation in progress — six local models (granite3-dense:8b, qwen3:4b-instruct, qwen3:1.7b, qwen3:4b, gemma4:e2b, gemma4:e4b) are currently being scored with Claude Opus as fixed judge. The ranking table and per-model reports will be published here.

Reports

Each run produces a self-contained HTML report — final score, per-category radar chart and the full item-by-item detail (question, model answer, grading).
